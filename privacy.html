Data Protection & Privacy

Privacy Notice & Artisan Data Protection Charter

HerVana is committed to ethical data stewardship. We treat personal data with dignity, prioritizing the safety, financial privacy, and identity protection of rural women artisans and conscious consumers.

📅 Effective: November 2026 • 🏛️ NRLM Fair Trade Certified • ✅ Legally Audited for Women SHGs
🔒

1. Rural Artisan Identity & Financial Safety

Rural women artisans face unique vulnerabilities, including predatory local lenders and middlemen. We implement stringent safeguards to ensure artisan data is never exposed.

  • Cooperative Identity Shield: Artisans may choose pseudonyms or group-level identifiers on public storefronts to protect individual personal safety.
  • Bank Details Encryption: Bank account numbers, IFSC codes, and PFMS identifiers are encrypted with AES-256 and never shared with patrons or third parties.
  • No Commercial Data Selling: We never sell, rent, monetize, or broker artisan or customer demographic data to advertisers or commercial data brokers.
💳

2. Patron & Buyer Transaction Security

When conscious consumers purchase handcrafted heritage goods on HerVana, their payment information is handled with the highest standard of banking security.

  • PCI-DSS Compliant Payment Gateways: Credit cards, UPI, and net banking transactions are processed via certified RBI-licensed payment gateways.
  • Card Tokenization: HerVana never stores raw credit or debit card numbers on our servers; transactions use RBI-approved device tokenization.
  • End-to-End SSL Encryption: All website traffic is secured with 256-bit TLS encryption, verified with official security certificates.
📋

3. Information We Collect & Why

We collect only the minimum necessary information required to fulfill orders, settle cooperative payouts, and deliver authentic village crafts to your doorstep.

  • Delivery Logistics: Customer name, shipping address, and phone number are used strictly by India Post or courier partners for parcel delivery.
  • SHG Registration: Group registration documents (NRLM ID, cooperative resolution, village bank passbook) are collected strictly for PFMS verification.
  • Privacy Rights & DPDP Act 2023: Users can request full export or deletion of their account data at any time by contacting privacy@hervana.org.
