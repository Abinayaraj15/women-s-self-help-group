Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions for SHGs

Does our SHG need a GST number to sell on HerVana?

Under Indian GST regulations for artisan handlooms and rural micro-cooperatives with annual turnover below ₹20 Lakhs, an SHG can register with their PAN and village panchayat certificate without GST.

How do customers receive products from remote rural villages?

HerVana has tied up with India Post (Speed Post) and leading rural logistics partners. When an order arrives, you pack it with the provided labels, and the local courier collects it directly from your village coordinator.

When and how does the group receive payment?

Payments are cleared directly into your registered cooperative bank account within 3 business days of the customer receiving the order, with zero intermediary deduction.
